Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. What we collect

  • Account info: your name, email address and profile photo from Google sign-in.
  • Requests & reports: anything you submit through the "Request a paper" or "Report a problem" forms.
  • Duel activity: your answers, scores and chat messages in 1v1 duels. Chat messages that are reported may be reviewed by staff.
  • Technical data: basic device/browser information needed to keep the site working securely, plus anonymous page-view counts (see “Cookies & analytics” below).

2. How we use it

  • To run your account, save your progress, and show you your stats, streaks and topic breakdown.
  • To show the leaderboard (unless you turn on "Hide me from the leaderboard" in your profile).
  • To respond to paper requests and question reports.
  • To keep the platform secure and prevent abuse.

3. Advertising

Some pages show ads, including through Google AdSense, which uses cookies to show relevant ads. Google ads are only loaded if you choose Accept in the cookie banner; if you choose Essential only, no Google ad cookies are set. You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer, and control ad personalisation through your Google account settings. We never show ads inside a running exam.

4. Cookies & analytics

We store a small amount of data in your browser to keep you signed in, remember your light/dark theme and remember your cookie choice. We count page views with a privacy-friendly analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not follow you across other sites.

5. Sharing

We don't sell your personal data. Your name and points may appear publicly on the leaderboard unless you opt out. We only share data with service providers (like our hosting and database provider) as needed to run the site, or where required by law.

6. Data retention

We keep your account and attempt history for as long as your account is active, so your progress and streaks stay intact. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time.

7. Your choices

  • Hide your name from the leaderboard from your Profile page.
  • Switch between light and dark theme; this is stored only in your browser.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data.

8. Children's use

A/L Master is aimed at students preparing for the G.C.E. Advanced Level exams. If you're using this site under the age required by your local law to consent on your own, please use it with a parent or guardian's involvement.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date above will change when we do.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent through the "Request a paper" form on your dashboard, or to the contact details shown in the footer of this site.
